# Stock Nexus SPI constraints

Review date: 2026-10-09 UTC. Read-only review of the pinned stock source and selected BOM. No firmware, settings, component, schematic, board or canonical repository changes were made. This packet does not qualify the unfinished routing or an installed binary.

## Result

At stock, unoverclocked 216 MHz core clock, the initialized ICM-42688-P bus runs at **13.5 MHz** and the initialized W25N01GVZEIG bus at **27 MHz**. There is **no nominal SPI clock-limit mismatch** against the cited device limits. SPI2 is already at the STM32F722's specified 27 MHz ceiling, however. Fast edge behavior, chip-select timing, receiver loading and real return paths still need review; these conclusions do not close the separate inherited I2C issues.

## Source and calculation

The target is [RDMS-NEXUS_F7 at 1d9cb4d](https://github.com/rotorflight/rotorflight-targets/blob/1d9cb4dc6f85c7895c2b089f4b8aa776ca3d3bd3/configs/RDMS-NEXUS_F7.config), blob ca042b1f51a341a46980cb9aba78420beab48be7. Its version header identifies [firmware ba6c7e3e328b91a549bf6ca87c9eadd7006b6d94](https://github.com/rotorflight/rotorflight-firmware/tree/ba6c7e3e328b91a549bf6ca87c9eadd7006b6d94). The F7 default overclock level is 0 (`common_pre.h`); startup uses PLL M=8, N=432, P=2 with 8 MHz HSE, APB1=/4 and APB2=/2. Thus PCLK1=54 MHz and PCLK2=108 MHz. Existing user settings, modified builds, oscillator tolerances and installed registers have not been inspected.

| Phase | Requested driver ceiling | Software divisor | Hardware prescaler | Actual nominal SCK |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| IMU detection, SPI1 | 1 MHz | 128 | 128 | 843.75 kHz |
| ICM42688 initialized, SPI1 | 24 MHz | 8 | 8 | 13.5 MHz |
| Flash detection/reset, SPI2 | 5 MHz | 32 | 16 | 3.375 MHz |
| W25N01GV initialized, SPI2 | 104 MHz | 2 | 2 | 27 MHz |

[`spiCalculateDivider`](https://github.com/rotorflight/rotorflight-firmware/blob/ba6c7e3e328b91a549bf6ca87c9eadd7006b6d94/src/main/drivers/bus_spi.c#L348) chooses power-of-two divisors from SystemCoreClock/2. [`spiDivisorToBRbits`](https://github.com/rotorflight/rotorflight-firmware/blob/ba6c7e3e328b91a549bf6ca87c9eadd7006b6d94/src/main/drivers/bus_spi_ll.c#L95) halves the requested divisor on SPI2/3, then clamps it to at least 2. The flash's software divisor 2 therefore becomes physical APB1/2, not /1. `spiCalculateClock(2)` alone returns 54 MHz and would overstate this SPI2 clock.

The [ICM driver](https://github.com/rotorflight/rotorflight-firmware/blob/ba6c7e3e328b91a549bf6ca87c9eadd7006b6d94/src/main/drivers/accgyro/accgyro_spi_icm426xx.c#L234) changes its divisor during initialization. The [W25N01G driver](https://github.com/rotorflight/rotorflight-firmware/blob/ba6c7e3e328b91a549bf6ca87c9eadd7006b6d94/src/main/drivers/flash_w25n01g.c#L917) changes its divisor after identification/reset. Its ordinary single-wire read uses command 03h with a dummy byte. The generic 20 MHz define/comment in `flash.c` is not the initialized W25N01GV limit. W25N01GV is NAND; a different NOR part's command-specific limit must not be substituted.

`calculate_clocks.py` reproduces this arithmetic; results are in `clock-calculations.json`. `stock-source-index.json` records exact upstream blob identities, SHA-256 values and links; all 14 retained firmware files and the target were verified against their upstream Git blob hashes.

## Selected parts and datasheet conditions

The authoritative `layout-revision/hardware/parts.json` identifies U1=STM32F722RET6, U2=ICM-42688-P and U3=W25N01GVZEIG. The inspected native board's pad identities agree with the stock resource map: IMU SCK/MISO/MOSI=PA5/PA6/PA7, CS=PA4, interrupt=PA15; flash SCK/MISO/MOSI=PB13/PB14/PB15, CS=PB12. The two buses are separate. `native-pad-identities.json` is bound to canonical69 / accepted69, SHA-256 `9cf04c88ebd31d7e2c12bdb8a9f80e01b615a7bdb94a73fc2234208a2e0a14c8`, by exact original/current PCB byte identity. It records terminal identities only, not continuity or routed length. R3/R4 are 10 kΩ CS pull-ups; R5/R6 are 10 kΩ flash WP/HOLD pull-ups. There are no series terminations on these listed signal nets.

- [TDK DS-000347 Rev 1.6](https://product.tdk.com/system/files/dam/doc/product/sensor/mortion-inertial/imu/data_sheet/ds-000347-icm-42688-p-v1.6.pdf), §3.5, p16: four-wire SPI maximum 24 MHz; high/low ≥17 ns; CS setup ≥39 ns and hold ≥18 ns; SDI setup/hold ≥13/8 ns; SDO valid ≤21.5 ns and hold ≥3.5 ns at 20 pF; SCLK rise/fall ≤16 ns. This is a characterization table based on five parts over voltage/temperature, introduced with 1.8 V/25°C conditions. It is not evidence of every assembled board at 3.3 V. The digital input capacitance `<10 pF` is in the typical column. Push-pull output levels are specified at a 1 MΩ load. DRIVE_CONFIG reset 05h selects `<2 ns` SPI output slew; the reviewed ICM driver does not override that register.
- [Winbond W25N01GV Rev Q, 2021-05-17](https://www.mouser.com/pdfDocs/W25N01GV_DS.pdf), §§9.4–9.6, pp58–61: 104 MHz applies to all instructions; input/output capacitances have 6/8 pF maxima; AC conditions use 30 pF load and ≤5 ns input transitions. Clock-to-output valid ≤7 ns; input setup/hold ≥2/3 ns; CS active setup/hold ≥5/3 ns; CS deselect ≥10 ns between array reads and ≥50 ns for the specified program/erase/status sequences. Observe its clock high/low and slew conditions as well. This is the manufacturer-authored datasheet at the authoritative BOM's URL. Winbond's [official selected-part page](https://www.winbond.com/hq/product/code-storage-flash/qspi-nand/w25n-gv/?__locale=ja&partNo=W25N01GVZEIG) confirms 104 MHz, 2.7–3.6 V and −40 to +85°C. Newer datasheet/silicon-lot applicability should be reconciled before fabrication; the official download endpoint did not yield the current PDF in this pass.
- [ST DS11853 Rev 9](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/stm32f722re.pdf), Table 81, pp161–162: SPI2/3 maximum is 27 MHz; SPI1 on PA5 is limited to 40 MHz. Master input setup/hold are ≥5/3 ns; master output valid is ≤3 ns. Conditions include OSPEEDR=11, 30 pF and 0.5 VDD timing references; values are guaranteed by characterization. Table 63 gives output transition maxima of 4 ns at 30 pF and 2.5 ns at 10 pF for VDD≥2.7 V. These are not minimum edge times. Table 61's 5 pF pin capacitance is typical, not a maximum.

## Constraints for the full-route review

1. Preserve the stock target, firmware, settings and selected parts. A CPU overclock would change these results and can put SPI2 above 27 MHz; do not assume a stock target file proves an installed device has default clocks. Read back first, without resetting settings.
2. Review SCK, MOSI, MISO and CS as complete driver-to-receiver nets, including package/pad loading, every via, branch, CS pull-up spur, probe/test stub and layer transition. Include IMU_INT and flash WP/HOLD state integrity. Confirm continuous nearby GND references, short return transitions and separation from switching nodes and high-current output paths using the final filled native copper.
3. The [stock GPIO configuration](https://github.com/rotorflight/rotorflight-firmware/blob/ba6c7e3e328b91a549bf6ca87c9eadd7006b6d94/src/main/drivers/bus_spi.h#L37) uses very-high-speed outputs. Clock repetition rate is not edge bandwidth. Assess reflection, overshoot/undershoot, crosstalk and repeated threshold crossings against actual fastest edges and stackup. No universal trace-length or length-matching cap is justified by this review. Do not add serpentine routing merely to match four SPI lengths.
4. Preserve the 20 pF IMU-SDO and 30 pF flash/MCU timing conditions as explicit review assumptions. Account for total external load, not trace capacitance alone. Typical MCU/IMU pin values cannot prove worst-case totals. Obtain suitable vendor bounds/models or leave the capacitance/edge qualification open; do not relabel an engineering estimate as a guaranteed limit.
5. Close setup and hold at receiver pins. For MISO the clock travels outward and data returns: include both route delays, device output-valid delay, threshold differences, jitter, duty distortion and edge settling. A nominal half-cycle calculation leaves 37.037−21.5−5=10.537 ns for IMU and 18.519−7−5=6.519 ns for flash before those effects. These are illustrative gross timing budgets, not certified margins or allowable route delays. Different manufacturer timing thresholds prevent directly treating the subtraction as qualification. MOSI and CS need their own budgets; frequency compliance does not prove CS setup/hold/deselect timing.
6. Separate inherited qualification gaps from layout changes. The stock MISO pull-up is enabled; ST specifies 30–50 kΩ on these pins. TDK's 1 MΩ output-level test does not establish a worst-case low-output bound against that stronger pull-up. This is an inherited guarantee gap requiring evidence/measurement, not evidence of observed failure and not a reason to silently alter firmware. SPI2's nominal ceiling and unknown installed settings are likewise inherited; excess new route loading, broken return paths or ringing are layout responsibilities.

## Bench acceptance still required

With motors disconnected and appropriate current limiting, record the installed binary/version, target/resource map, core/APB clocks, SPI BR/CPOL/CPHA, GPIO speed/pulls and IMU drive setting. Verify the detection and initialized clocks. Probe with known low-capacitance probes at both ends; include probe load in the assessment. Exercise initialization, sustained gyro reads and flash command/data transfers through both polled and DMA paths. Check actual CS intervals, receiver setup/hold, levels, transitions, ringing and errors over intended supply/temperature and representative regulator/output activity. Check both data directions and flash status/write/readback behavior using a safe designated test region. A source review, nominal clock screen or DRC pass alone supplies no flight or fabrication qualification.
