# Power model scope and servo supply limits

This is a scope correction and component evidence summary, not a native0 DC result. Fresh selected-source DC is pending. Source1 numerical results remain historical.

The preserved model defines **294 required accessory/logic/USB cases** and **11 servo illustrations**. The required count does not qualify servo loads. Its fixed allocation vertices are not continuous-current ratings. The source1 servo illustrations use one ideal 7.4 V BEC with unmeasured feed resistance assumptions; the model does not reproduce actual BEC limiting, regulation, thermal behavior or installed harness losses.

Six heavier source1 servo illustrations require approximately 11.093-11.124 A or 18.126-18.561 A total. All exceed the documented 10 A continuous reference for **Hobbywing Platinum 200A HV SBEC V4.1**; five nevertheless pass the ideal-source board-pad voltage screen. That pass does not establish BEC capability. The V4.1 manual specifies 5-8 V output, 10 A continuous and 30 A peak; its two feed leads share one internal BEC. The 30 A peak figure establishes no common pulse duration, repetition or installation thermal envelope. The actual installed ESC revision remains to be identified. [Manufacturer V4.1 manual](https://www.hobbywing.com/en/uploads/file/20221015/a728560821bb05490636413c693a115f.pdf)

The intended KST BLS815 cyclic and BLS805X/BLS905X tail servos have a **6.0-8.4 V** manufacturer supply range. **12.6 V raw servo operation is excluded.** The raw servo bus bypasses U5; the electronics branch clamp/current limit is not servo-bus overvoltage or total-current protection. Plotted 4.5/2.3/2.4 A comparison endpoints have duration/temperature limits and are not continuous helicopter-load ratings. Exact installed servo revisions remain to be bound. [BLS815 specification](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0570/1766/3541/files/BLS815_V8.0_Technical_Specification.pdf?v=1700473913), [BLS805X specification](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0570/1766/3541/files/BLS805X_Technical_Specification.pdf?v=1713150470), [BLS905X specification](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0570/1766/3541/files/BLS905X_Technical_Specification.pdf?v=1709113577).

The selected header is Samtec HTSW-101-08-L-T-RA. Published current figures belong to specified mated combinations and conditions; they do not establish the rating of unknown RC female contacts, crimps and wires. Actual mates, harness construction, sharing, lost-feed behavior, output servo-terminal voltage, duty cycle, temperature and derating remain unqualified. Two positive feeds, return pins and signal pins do not create extra BEC power capacity. [Manufacturer connector catalog](https://suddendocs.samtec.com/catalog_english/htsw_th.pdf)
