# Small candidate01: bounded electrical review

The exact native candidate has no new component-value or DC-topology contradiction in the four reviewed circuits. This supports a conditional prototype screen, not an electrical, ESD, AC, fabrication, or flight qualification. DSM's perimeter detour is the principal new signal-integrity question: it is on the **136.672265 mm R38-to-MCU signal path**, not a harmless long pullup spur. The actual R71 spur is only 3.234807 mm. No universal route-length limit is asserted.

This review binds the saved/refilled PCB SHA-256 `14dea1df09ea9d800bf66a6d74eb5f0dac33a8705161e9ed1a02f2e11f3b58b8`, its native export, and its construction/proposal receipts. The replay checks all 21 added polylines and 13 vias against actual native records, all 75 removed records, terminal nets, unchanged values, and R44's in-place 180° rotation. Only this review directory was written. Parent-owned geometry/return/power gates and adoption are separate.

## DSM: actual path, source, load, and settings

Native/BOM topology is external receiver at J12.3 → DSM_RX_EXT, with D7.1 shunted through D7 (onsemi ESD9M5.0ST5G) to D7.2/GND → R38.1 → R38 (22 Ω ±1%) → R38.2/DSM_RX_MCU → U1.43/PA10. R71 (100 kΩ ±1%, ROM RX idle) connects +3V3_CORE to the MCU-side net. Both external-net tracks reach the D7 signal pad; D7 is before the series resistor. J12.1 supplies +3V3_DSM and J12.2 is GND. The selected external receiver, cable, output impedance, voltage levels, and fastest edges are not specified by this BOM.

Paths are sums of actual track centerlines, with via crossings connected but vertical barrel length and travel through pads excluded. Each main-path segment UUID is retained in arithmetic.json. The long main path has approximately 0.9085 Ω nominal trace resistance and 0.850–0.860 ns one-way continuous-plane line-model delay, excluding via/return detours. The 3.23 mm resistively terminated spur is not the whole loading story: every through-via barrel/pad and unused barrel section also matters at fast edges.

Pinned Rotorflight firmware `ba6c7e3e328b91a549bf6ca87c9eadd7006b6d94` [spektrum.h](https://github.com/rotorflight/rotorflight-firmware/blob/ba6c7e3e328b91a549bf6ca87c9eadd7006b6d94/src/main/rx/spektrum.h#L36) sets 115200 baud. The [driver](https://github.com/rotorflight/rotorflight-firmware/blob/ba6c7e3e328b91a549bf6ca87c9eadd7006b6d94/src/main/rx/spektrum.c) plus UART HAL resolve ordinary DSM receive to 8N1, MODE_RX; reset defaults are uninverted and halfDuplex=0. The pinned target maps SERIAL_RX 1=A10, but does not select the active serial receiver provider/port. Therefore 115200 is conditional on selecting this DSM driver; installed firmware, configuration, ROM-loader behavior, and alternative protocols are unverified. A bit is 8.681 µs; this does not bound edge speed.

Ordinary PA10 RX initialization uses AF7, no internal pull, and LOW speed. That output-speed setting does not slow an external receiver's incoming edge. Conditional startup binding temporarily drives the receive pin push-pull LOW speed, with 120 µs low/high pulses after a 60 ms delay. R38 then lies at the far end of the long PCB trace rather than at the MCU source. R38 is near the connector for incoming traffic, but the external driver/cable precede it, and 22 Ω plus unknown source impedance is not established matched termination. The distinct correct PA10 IBIS selector/model is retained: `io8usb_id_otg_ft` / `io8p00_id_otg_ft`; do not reuse PC14's model. Its ramp data are simulation fixtures, not a guaranteed installed edge bound.

The complete MCU-side tree has a **12.88–15.14 pF trace-only sensitivity scale**. [ST DS11853 Rev9](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/stm32f722re.pdf), Table61, gives 5 pF typical pin capacitance, not a maximum. [onsemi Rev9](https://www.onsemi.com/download/data-sheet/pdf/esd9m5.0s-d.pdf), p2, gives D7 maximum 2.5 pF at 0 V/1 MHz and 25°C. Adding these to the outer-net trace scale gives roughly 20.8–23.1 pF, **before all six vias, pads, actual cable/receiver loading and probes**. This mixed typical/conditional sum is not a guaranteed total. If using IBIS, combine C_comp and package terms consistently rather than adding the datasheet CIO again. Do not treat a TVS capacitance rating or correct terminal topology as proof of clamping effectiveness.

R71 is an idle bias, not demonstrated fast-edge drive. For assumed total loads of 20/50/100 pF, R71 at +1% would take 2.43/6.08/12.16 µs to reach 0.7VDD from zero; these are illustrative lumped RC cases, not measured capacitances or guarantees. Even the 50 pF case is a substantial fraction of a 115200-baud bit, so open-drain/tri-state behavior cannot be accepted from the baud rate alone. During ordinary push-pull reception the receiver dominates. A 25°C powered-idle example with MCU 1 µA plus D7 1 µA leakage drops 0.202 V across 101 kΩ at 3.3 V; external receiver leakage, negative injection, hot leakage, and unpowered backfeed remain unbounded.

Before relying on this route, check the actual receiver/cable, both signal endpoints and bind direction with characterized low-capacitance probes, including startup, unplug/replug, and power sequencing. Record levels, fastest edges, overshoot/undershoot, threshold recrossings and frame errors during flash/servo/regulator activity. Verify plane apertures and local reference transfer at B→In2, F→In3 and In3→In2 transitions. A sub-nanosecond nominal flight time and microsecond bit time do not by themselves settle reflection, coupling, return, or ESD questions.

## FLASH HOLD/WP: static straps with transient limits

Actual U3 is Winbond **W25N01GVZEIG NAND**, not a substituted NOR part. R5/R6 remain 10 kΩ ±1% from +3V3_CORE to U3.3 /WP and U3.7 /HOLD. Complete /WP copper is 12.602393 mm with two vias. Complete /HOLD copper is 21.273885 mm with two vias; the 17.982801 mm recipe value excludes retained pad approaches.

The pinned target's flash_spi_bus=2 selects ordinary FLASHIO_SPI in flash.c. The [W25N driver](https://github.com/rotorflight/rotorflight-firmware/blob/ba6c7e3e328b91a549bf6ca87c9eadd7006b6d94/src/main/drivers/flash_w25n01g.c) uses ordinary read 03h on that path; 6Bh quad read is in the separate QUADSPI branch. Reset clears protection/WP-E. These two nets are therefore static pullups in the reviewed stock configuration. This does not authorize assuming a different installed binary or quad wiring.

[Winbond RevQ](https://www.mouser.com/pdfDocs/W25N01GV_DS.pdf), §§4.3–4.4 and9.4: /HOLD low while selected pauses standard/dual transfers; /WP protection depends on status settings. At maximum 2 µA leakage and 10.1 kΩ, static droop is 20.2 mV, leaving 0.7898 V above the 0.7VCC high threshold at VCC=2.7 V. Trace resistance is negligible in that DC screen. Pin capacitance maxima are 6 pF input/8 pF output under the datasheet conditions; trace-only scales are 1.25 pF for /WP and 1.87–2.42 pF for /HOLD, with vias/pads/coupling omitted. Assumed total 10/25/50 pF loads give 0.12/0.30/0.61 µs to reach 0.7VCC through10.1 kΩ after an ideal supply step. Actual rail ramp, first chip-select timing, and coupled glitches are unverified. Preserve the pullups and check HOLD/WP startup/traffic waveforms; there is no synchronous data-length matching requirement established for these straps.

## ADC: unchanged divider, new MID and local ground geometry

R42=68.0 kΩ ±0.1% goes from +5V_PERIPH to ADC_BUS/U1.10/PC2. R43 and R44 are each16.0 kΩ ±0.1% in series from ADC_BUS through ADC_DIV_MID to GND. C31 remains220 nF/16 V/X7R directly from ADC_BUS to GND. This is a 0.32 divider, 21.76 kΩ Thevenin resistance and nominal4.7872 ms/33.246 Hz low-pass. At5 V the divider draws50 µA and outputs1.6 V; resistor-only gain extremes are0.319565–0.320435. These are unchanged component-level results, not accuracy qualification.

Full ADC_BUS copper is17.399669 mm with2 vias; its retained C31-to-MCU track records match candidate54 exactly. New R43-to-R44 MID is13.584103 mm F/In2/F with2 vias and0.09085 Ω nominal track resistance. Its roughly1.25–1.48 pF trace scale is on a different node from C31 and must not simply be added directly to C31. No capacitor loading bound or coupling simulation is claimed.

R44 rotates from90° to−90° at the same(25.8,16.0) center. Its new0.25 mm-wide1.025329 mm ground lead reaches its own new through via at(25.08,15.78); no other GND track ends at that via. Nominal trace resistance is2.02 mΩ and trace-only static drop at50 µA is0.101 µV. This removes the former local shared-via connection from this branch; the wider ground network remains shared. In the low-frequency ideal divider, R44 ground offset couples to ADC_BUS with coefficient0.68 relative to the ADC reference ground, so the small calculated lead drop does not bound real ground-noise error.

Retained stock ADC sources select PCLK/8,480-cycle sampling and12-bit conversion. Conditional216 MHz HCLK/APB2÷2 gives35.556 µs acquisition. Using the datasheet maxima RADC=6 kΩ and CADC=7 pF, +0.1% Rth and an explicit2 Ω series sensitivity yields1.887 µs simple quarter-LSB settling. This limited RC screen is favorable; it is not a sampled-network simulation or installed-register check. A separate worst-case charge-sharing approximation requires effective C31≈114.7 nF for quarter-LSB error;220 nF nominal does not guarantee that value under bias/temperature. Full-scale external-filter settling to that error level is nominal46.46 ms, distinct from one ADC acquisition. Recheck effective capacitance, VDDA/VREF, calibration, startup/large steps and coupled noise with the actual ground/reference geometry.
