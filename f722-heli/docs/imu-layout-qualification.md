# IMU layout qualification remains open

This qualification note is bound to the frozen final prototype PCB SHA-256 `4da0708a4795662cc32b035a0783c70b9bdb85fc386bd4d39af33385b175667f`. A fresh KiCad 10.0.6 native comparison on 2026-10-07 confirms the relevant U2 geometry against accepted1 PCB `f56e26bd33974daeba215012b1adb77789b21c219e79c35de93aaf0bce5caa32`. This documentation update changes no copper, placement, source pin mapping or design rule.

U2 remains ICM-42688-P on F.Cu at **(23.209999, 11.600000) mm, rotation 0°**. The exact-board comparison finds no changed pad, track or via geometry in the nominal body, maximum envelope, engineering reserve or central window; filled-zone symmetric-difference area is **0.0 mm² on every layer in every window**, with no drill holes in those windows. This establishes geometric carry-forward, not manufacturer layout compliance.

U2's mounting-face central area and the existing via/hole reserve are clear. That check is narrower than a multilayer copper-avoidance assessment. In the nominal 3.0 × 2.5 mm package footprint, In1 and In4 each still contain **7.5 mm² of GND**. In2, In3 and B retain power or signal routing. The buried planes remain reference and return conductors; they are not an all-layer keepout.

[TDK's current AN-000393 revision 2.4, dated 2026-02-09](https://d17t6iyxenbwp1.cloudfront.net/s3fs-public/2026-06/AN-000393%20TDK%20InvenSense%20IMU%20PCB%20Design%20and%20MEMS%20Assembly%20Guidelines%20v2.4.pdf?VersionId=0JJT_E0010fzx6pG58sIeAXICnPVAVgn), §2.1 p6, retains guidance to avoid under-device vias, traces and copper pour. Its §2.4 pp8–9 discusses mounting stress and illustrates outward fanout. Revision 2.0 explicitly extended the avoidance recommendation to all layers. Revision 2.4 omits that explicit phrase, but its revision history gives neither a reason for the change nor an endorsement of buried copper. This is a design recommendation, not an electrical absolute-maximum rating; no measured sensor fault is established by geometry alone.

The existing F722 layout therefore retains an unresolved sensor-layout qualification question. No vendor-compliance or sensor-noise/stress guarantee is claimed. A conservative full-body multilayer interpretation would require coordinated rerouting of the CORE, IMU/SPI, LED, servo, Port C and flash conductors, together with local apertures in both ground planes. Removing plane copper while leaving the present signal paths would remove their reference. A central-only exclusion would remain a partial implementation, not full-body avoidance.

The [exact-board IMU audit](../evidence/imu-final95-review/REPORT.md) records the native comparison, with the retained manufacturer source identified in its source manifest. The fresh board comparison does not imply a newly fetched manufacturer document. Any corrective layout must repeat native connectivity/DRC, all-layer body checks, actual ground-return and power analysis, and sensor/assembly qualification. Surface clearance, static power screens and routing counts do not resolve this separate qualification question.
