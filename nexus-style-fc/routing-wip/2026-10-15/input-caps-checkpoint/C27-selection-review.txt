C27 input-capacitance gate and remedy decision
15 October 2026. Read-only engineering screen; no design, BOM, vendor, or GitHub changes.

CAPACITOR CHANGE APPROVED; PHYSICAL FEASIBILITY PENDING
No single 0603 replacement was qualified in this bounded screen. For a margin-first proposal, replace C27 with Samsung CL21A226MAYNNNE / JLC-LCSC C602037 (22 µF, 25 V, ±20%, X5R, 0805) and add one identical capacitor in parallel locally at U14 VIN/GND. This changes the C27 footprint and adds one new capacitor/reference. The user has approved this capacitor change; an actual fit/layout check remains pending. An additional D5 move has not been approved. It preserves the 25 V capacitor rating. The pair has materially more screening margin than a one-part nominal-value substitution.

This is a defensible design proposal, NOT a guaranteed manufacturer minimum or board-level signoff. A separate read-only placement check is underway. The capacitor-change approval does not establish a compact feasible local loop or authorize an additional D5 move. Final acceptance still requires the actual BEC envelope and service-life qualification; do not treat either the 25% example or 8.4 V point as an acceptance criterion.

VERIFIED CURRENT GATE
TI TPS629210 non-Q1 Rev B §7.3 specifies 3 µF minimum effective input capacitance. §11.1 requires a short local VIN/GND loop and a VIN-tied EN connection directly at the input capacitor. Current native netlist confirms C27=C69335 and C18=C52923 are the only capacitors directly spanning VSYS; U14 VIN and EN are on VSYS. VSYS is after D5, so a nominal 7.4 V BEC does not mean exactly 7.4 V at C27.

Across illustrative 6.8–7.4 V VSYS, C27's exact Samsung typical DC-bias curve gives only 1.801–1.641 µF. Even the arithmetic total with C18 is only 2.078–1.892 µF typical; this total is not credited as local high-frequency CIN. Thus the current evidence cannot substantiate TI's 3 µF requirement. The chosen 6.8–7.4 V range is a screen, not a measured BEC tolerance/drop/transient specification.

C602037 EVIDENCE AND LIMITS
Samsung lists CL21A226MAYNNNE as mass production and the recommended replacement for NRND CL21A226MAQNNNE/C45783. Exact reference specification dated 13 November 2025: 22 µF ±20%,25 V,X5R ±15% over −55 to +85 °C; body 2.00±0.20 ×1.25±0.20 ×1.25±0.20 mm. Current C27 is 1.60±0.15 ×0.80±0.15 ×0.80±0.15 mm: the proposal requires 0805 land patterns and up to 1.45 mm height rather than 0.95 mm. It is not a drop-in for the current C_0603_1608Metric footprint. Do not assume enclosure/courtyard clearance.

MANUFACTURER LAND-PATTERN CHECK
Exact C602037 specsheet PDF p.33 §5-4, metric2012 size with±0.20 mm chip tolerance: pad-to-pad gap a=0.83–0.93 mm, each pad length b=0.91–1.01 mm, pad width c=1.35–1.45 mm; overall copper length a+2b=2.65–2.95 mm. Diagram and row were visually verified. The standard KiCad0805 pad1.00×1.45 mm at±0.95 mm centers has0.90 mm gap and lies within these ranges. There is no manufacturer basis for squeezing Y pad width to≤1.20 mm. Body maximum width itself is1.45 mm; required courtyard and assembly tolerances remain separate. A placement worker reported fixed-copper/courtyard conflicts in the current pocket, so physical feasibility must be resolved with that review rather than assuming a custom undersized footprint is acceptable. EXACT BEC CONTEXT AND SCREENING BASIS
Hobbywing Platinum 120 A V4, SKU 30203401, is the established ESC, used on6S. Its official manual specifies a 5–8 V BEC setting range in 0.1 V steps,6 V default,10 A continuous/25 A peak. The intended7.4 V setting is valid but is not verified as actually programmed. The0.1 V step is programming resolution, not voltage accuracy/tolerance. The manual requires saving settings and powering the ESC off/on for them to take effect. The separate ESC120 A/150 A rating has a 10 s peak annotation in the 2016 manual/product page; do not transfer that duration to the BEC 25 A rating.

No numerical BEC output accuracy/tolerance, ripple limit, startup/load-step overshoot ceiling, or duration for the BEC 25 A peak was found in the reviewed official product pages, brochure and 2016/2018 manuals. This is a documented information gap, not evidence of zero overshoot or unlimited peak duration. Raw 6S must never feed the FC.

The2018 manual§04.2 recommends the stock external capacitor pack in parallel at the BEC output for sudden servo loads beyond its capability; the wiring drawing labels it10 V/4700 µF. This is separate remote bulk support, not a published transient ceiling or qualification of local C27. Do not credit it as a compact U14 input capacitor.

Meaningful nominal DC screen: cover the published 5–8 V BEC setpoint range and credit NO diode drop at the upper end.8.0 V is therefore the conservative nominal-setpoint screening point. It is not a measured or guaranteed maximum voltage at VSYS; regulation tolerance and transients can exceed a programmed setpoint.10 V and 12 V below are additional sensitivity points only, not asserted transient bounds.7.4 V remains the intended user setting. Confirm actual output after configuration and validate VSYS at the populated board during startup/hot plug and worst BEC/servo/load steps.

MANUFACTURER AGING GUIDANCE
The exact C602037 manual (PDF pp.13–14) says Class II capacitance falls with log(time),  varies by model, and is recovered by 150 °C/1 h heat treatment, after which aging restarts. It supplies no exact-part numerical aging rate. Samsung’s official FAQ illustrates a GENERIC TYPICAL X5R/X7R slope of 2–5% per decade-hour with C(t)=C0[1−k log10(t)]. The table’s analytical scenario selects the upper illustrated5%/decade and 10 years from a 1 h reference, giving factor0.75286. Ten years is an explicit assumption, not a supplied requirement; the generic typical slope is not an exact-part upper bound, and the DC-bias curve’s aging reference is unspecified. This supports a conservative direction but cannot certify lifetime. Exact-part/lot combined-corner data or measured validation remains necessary.

FINAL VALIDATION LIMITS
The actual BEC tolerance/transient envelope and intended service life remain release gates. Also check the compact local VIN/GND loop, VIN-tied EN connection at the capacitors, source impedance/added-capacitance inrush, ripple heating, small-signal capacitance, lot and temperature corners. Capacitor surface temperature must remain within−55 to+85 °C including self-heating. TPS629210 permits17 V maximum recommended VIN and 18 V absolute maximum; capacitor25 V rating does not extend those IC limits. This study does not qualify the pair across the converter’s full 3–17 V operating range or guarantee any unbounded transient.
