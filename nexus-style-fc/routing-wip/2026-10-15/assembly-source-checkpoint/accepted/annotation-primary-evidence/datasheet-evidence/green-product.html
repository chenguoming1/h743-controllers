XL-1608SYGC-04

Chengxingguang offers a full line of small size high quality front Front chip LED, featuring high light efficiency, low power consumption and RoHS standards.


The front patch LED series is available in regular brightness and high brightness. It is packaged with AlGaAs, AlInGaP and InGaN chips provided by well-known chip manufacturers, with a full range of colors. The products are designed with industrial standard pins and are available with patches, inverted and side sticker welding structures. The colors include green, red, yellow, white, blue, orange, warm white, and a variety of colors of two-color and multi-color Colorful./RGB Choose.


Patch LED lamp beads are widely used, including medical care applications, automotive interior, indicator lights, LCD and button backlights, household appliances, instruments, industrial control systems, computer peripherals and consumer electronics status indications, etc.


Due to its low power consumption, small size and easy assembly, patch LED beads make it very common in smart phones and industrial backlight displays.

Product Characteristics
  • Outline Dimensions (L / w / h): 1.6*0.8*0.4 mm

  • Luminous color and colloid: Yellow green / water  colloid

  • Environmental protection products Complied With ROHS Directive

  • Moisture sensitivity level（MSL）: 3 levels

  • EIA standard packaging

  • Suitable for SMT automatic production

  • Suitable for infrared reflow soldering process
Product Application
  • Medical equipment: endoscope 、oximeter

  • Automotive electronics: backlight key light 、 indicator light

  • Industrial products: electronic instrument panel、 industrial equipment

  • Smart home: white appliances, nixie tube LED

  •  Communication products: mobile phone button lamp, router
