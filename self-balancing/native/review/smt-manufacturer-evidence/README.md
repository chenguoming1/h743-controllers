# SMT manufacturer comparisons and controlled analysis

The component comparisons in u2, d4, u11 and connectors were made against the frozen P3 board. They preserve that source evidence. Later controlled JLCDFM tests established that the U2 and U11 overlap reds depend on the split paste-window representation, while D4's exact ST rectangular pattern still receives a generic yellow overlap report. See controlled-diagnostic-disposition.json and the two diagnostic evidence folders for current results.

P4 retains the original U2 and U11 production split stencils. It changes D4 to the exact ST rectangular land bounds and corrects the two USB front slots to the HRO recommended hole length, with the existing 0.20 mm annular ring. The earlier D4 rounded-corner and USB 1.20 mm slot measurements are therefore historical comparisons, not P4 production dimensions.

No diagnostic Gerber archive is included here. Diagnostic projects are not manufacturing packages, and their reduced warning counts must not be represented as a pass for the production stencil. The final P4 production rerun is recorded separately.
