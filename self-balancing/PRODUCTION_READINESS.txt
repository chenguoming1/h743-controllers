R3-S6-P6 production instructions

Use only the final P6 release files and their SHA-256 manifest. All earlier
fabrication packages are superseded. P5 contained confirmed narrow copper
attachments and must not be fabricated.

PCB selections
- 38 x 38 mm, six layers, nominal 1.6 mm, JLC06161H-3313.
  Published finished thickness: 1.54 mm +/-10%.
- 1 oz outer / 0.5 oz inner copper; ENIG; green mask; white silk.
- Controlled-impedance USB pairs: 90 ohms, 0.1392 mm width and 0.1600 mm gap,
  front referenced to L2 and rear referenced to L5. Confirm the named stack
  and impedance requirement in the manufacturer's CAM production files.
- Select the documented 0.20 mm drill / small-via process. Seven vias use
  0.35 mm copper diameter; all other vias are at least 0.40 mm.
- Epoxy-fill, copper-cap and mask-cover the 0.20 mm vias. Keep the USB shell
  component slots and the NPTH mounting/locating holes open. Confirm this
  treatment in CAM. The selected process may affect the quote.
- Preserve the four plated USB shell slots: two 0.60 x 1.40 mm and two
  0.60 x 1.70 mm. Do not replace them with round holes.

Before authorizing assembly
- Use Standard double-sided PCBA with an approved carrier, tooling and
  fiducials. Determine connector support/fixture needs during production
  engineering. A newer live J3 catalog capture had no fixture notice; an
  older cached warning is not a confirmed mandatory charge. Preserve
  connector mouth, nozzle and depaneling access.
- Review the final supplier TWO-FACE 2D production placement drawing.
  Match physical pin-1/cathode marks to native numbered pads and the exact
  79-row corrected CPL. A symmetric outline fit or a generic 3D red dot
  is not a polarity proof. The native audit CPL is reference data; submit
  JLC_CPL.csv, which preserves the user's supplier corrections.
- Include and inspect soldering all four J2 S1 plated shell anchors.
  Their paste openings are intentionally absent; contacts-only reflow
  does not establish mechanical shell joints.

Stencil request for production engineering
JLC regenerates stencil data, so enter these requirements in order remarks:
Review a top-side 0.12 mm base stencil with a 0.10 mm U13 region and a
0.10 mm bottom stencil. Preserve U2's eight 0.50 x 0.25 mm R0.05 lead
apertures and two 0.90 x 0.70 mm R0.05 exposed-pad windows. Preserve U11's
four 1.37 x 1.73 mm R0.25 exposed-pad windows. Do not replace split windows
with full exposed-pad openings. Confirm the actual step boundary, nearby
aperture clearance and deposited volumes, or identify a qualified
alternative for approval. This is a feasible review proposal, not an
already-approved stencil. Inspect the hidden solder joints.

First article
Use current-limited initial power and inspect shorts/polarity first. Test
rails, source switching, HSE, USB, IMU, NAND and external interfaces. Verify
the actual J3 harness cavity-to-pad mapping before applying power. Validate
thermal loading, connector/stack access and the required USB attach/current
firmware policy. Compilation alone is not firmware or flight qualification.

These notes specify production requirements. They are not an order, supplier
production approval, measured impedance certificate or hardware test result.
